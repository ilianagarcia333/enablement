What I’d build as Director

Our customers and our teams are busy. They won’t go looking for training, so it has to come to them, built for their role and current with the product.

Role-based learning paths

A clear path for every Sales, CX, and Support role, built with each team’s leadership so nothing a role needs is missing.

Certification worth earning

Product knowledge certification, plus a path to certify people who train others. With real rewards: I designed the pins, patches, t-shirts, and jackets for our training program at Verano, and incentives work.

Release readiness

A shared release sheet where each team tests what’s changing and signs off before it reaches customers.

Learning in every format

Everyone learns differently. Video, in-app help, quick references, live sessions, and shadowing, chosen on purpose for each audience.

First 90 days

  • Map what each role needs with team leads
  • Audit training against the current product
  • Launch the release sign-off sheet

Months 3 to 6

  • Roll out learning paths for Sales, CX, and Support
  • Choose an LMS that scales, starting with internal teams

Months 6 to 12

  • Launch certification with incentives
  • Report ramp time, completion, and readiness to leadership

What success would need

An LMS that can grow with us, a regular rhythm of communication across teams, partnership from team leaders, and ownership of training decisions so programs can move as fast as the product does.
